CareFlow AI Walkthrough

CareFlow AI · International Patient Operations Platform

One connected journey for every international patient

CareFlow connects the first enquiry and medical documents to clinical review, treatment proposals, travel arrangements and post-treatment follow-up. It is designed to work alongside a hospital's existing management system, not replace it.

Working product demonstration. Not yet approved for real patient records.

What it is, and what it is not

CareFlow is not

  • An AI document reader
  • Another hospital management system
  • A patient registration app
  • A generic CRM
  • A chatbot for hospitals

The operations layer for an international patient department

It handles the work that happens around the hospital system: from the first message to the last follow-up. AI does the slow reading and translating. Coordinators and doctors make every decision, and the hospital system stays the system of record.

How everyone connects

Every person works on one shared case. The patient reaches it through a private link with no account. Staff reach it through their role. The audit log, document storage and AI sit underneath.

The patient journey, step by step

A case moves from the patient's first message through reading, triage and clinical review, then on to the proposal, appointment, travel and follow-up. The patient's tracking link stays open the whole time.

PatientHospital staffAutomatic
EnquiryA four-step form in the patient's language, with three consents. The patient gets a reference and a private link.
AI reads documentsText is extracted, translated into English, summarised and structured. Only if the hospital enabled AI and the patient consented.
Triage and reviewThe coordinator corrects the AI, signs off translations and sends the case to a doctor, who approves a recommendation.
Proposal to follow-upA clinically approved proposal, an appointment with the hospital's own booking reference, travel details, then follow-ups until closure.

How a hospital gets started

Each hospital is a separate workspace with its own staff, cases, settings and storage. Setup takes one admin about fifteen minutes.

What is in the product today

Patient intake and tracking

  • Five languages, including Arabic right-to-left
  • Hospital logo and colours
  • PDF, JPG and PNG uploads
  • Private tracking link with status, messages and uploads

Case management

  • Case reference per hospital and year
  • Status, priority, coordinator and doctor
  • Next-step guidance and full timeline
  • Saved views, bulk assignment, CSV export

Documents and AI

  • Text extraction, language detection, classification
  • Translation, summary, structured medical facts
  • Side-by-side viewer with zoom and PDF paging
  • Human review and translation sign-off

Clinical review

  • Observations and recommendations
  • Doctor approval; nurses cannot approve
  • Doctors see only their own cases
  • Personal review queue

Coordination

  • Treatment proposals with patient response
  • Appointments with the hospital's booking reference
  • Travel, pickup and accommodation
  • Tasks and follow-ups

Communication

  • Email from editable message templates
  • Approval step before sending
  • Notification bell for new work
  • Automations that can be switched on or off

Hospital administration

  • Staff, seat limits, departments, roles
  • Modules and email settings
  • CareFlow storage or the hospital's own bucket
  • Retention, patient export and erasure

Privacy and security

  • Every hospital fully separated
  • Two-factor login and invitation-only sign-up
  • AI off by default; needs hospital and patient consent
  • Audit log of every document opened
